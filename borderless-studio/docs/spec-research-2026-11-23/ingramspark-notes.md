# IngramSpark research notes

Research date: 2026-11-23. Context: publisher in Sydney, Australia; paperback, hardcover and ebook; intended wholesale discount 30%.

Files in this folder:

- `facts.json` - structured facts. Every fact has `value`, `source` (exact URL), `quote` (verbatim from the saved text) and `verified`. Start with `quick_reference`, then the topic sections.
- `notes.md` - this file.
- `book_types_full.json` - all 984 book types from the public tool data (id, description, trim, paper, binding, laminate, min/max pages). The compact per-trim table is inside `facts.json`.
- `pages/` - plain text of every page and PDF relied on (104 files, first line = URL).
- `raw/` - downloaded PDFs, HTML, JSON and calculator responses. `scripts/` - the scripts that built the files.

## 1. How the research was done

- Fetching: `curl -sL` through the sandbox proxy. PDFs converted with `pdftotext -layout`; HTML with lxml.
- Reachable: `www.ingramspark.com` (marketing pages, blog, `/hubfs` PDFs) and `myaccount.ingramspark.com` (the four public tools and the JSON endpoints behind them, no login).
- NOT reachable: `help.ingramspark.com`. Every request returned HTTP 403 with `cf-mitigated: challenge` (Cloudflare bot challenge), with curl and with the WebFetch tool; the Zendesk API returned 401. I did not try to get around it. No help-centre article was read. `help.lightningsource.com` behaves the same. `www.lightningsource.com` redirects to `www.ingramcontent.com` (no specifications there).
- Dead links found: `https://www.ingramspark.com/plan-your-book/print/file-creation` (404), `https://myaccount.ingramspark.com/documents/IngramSparkPriceSheet.pdf` (404, still linked from the FAQ as "pricing and services guide"), `https://myaccount.ingramspark.com/documents/contracts/ICSPRK_INTEBOOK.pdf` (404).
- WebSearch was used only to discover official URLs (it found the User Guide and the 2026 rate card). Every fact was then read from the official file itself.
- Quote checking: `facts.json` is generated by `scripts/build_facts.py`. Each of the 767 quotes was matched by program against the saved page text (whitespace runs collapsed; curly and straight quotes treated alike) and the stored quote is the text of the saved page. A second script (`scripts/validate_facts.py`) re-checked the hand-typed tables (bleed chart, spine samples, price checks) against the raw data: 0 mismatches.
- No login, no account, no form that needs an account. The Cover Template Generator was not submitted (it needs an ISBN and an email address).

## 3. Headline findings (details and quotes in facts.json)

- **Interior bleed:** 0.125 in (3 mm) on top, bottom and outside edge only; none on the bind edge. Page with bleed = (trim width + 0.125) x (trim height + 0.25). Without bleed: exact trim size.
- **Interior margins:** 0.5 in (13 mm) on all sides, worded as a recommendation. For colour books Ingram also recommends a 0.125 in blank strip at the bind edge. Trim tolerance is 1/16 in (2 mm).
- **Page count:** minimum 18; the stored page count is always even; the last page carries Ingram's barcode/manufacturing information. Maximum 840 to 1200 depending on paper and binding.
- **PDF:** PDF/X-1a:2001 or PDF/X-3:2002; all fonts embedded; single pages, not spreads; no crop or registration marks; no spot colours; no ICC profiles; one PDF for the whole interior; up to 1.5 GB.
- **Images:** 300 ppi (600 ppi for 1-bit line art). B&W interiors in grayscale; colour interiors and covers in CMYK.
- **Ink:** total coverage at most 240%; rich black 60/40/40/100; no registration black; text of 24 pt or smaller in 100% K.
- **Cover:** 0.125 in bleed on all four sides; type safety 0.25 in recommended (template allows 0.125 in); no spine text under 48 pages for paperbacks; spine safety 0.0625 in (spine 0.35 in or wider) or 0.03125 in (narrower).
- **Barcode:** mandatory; 100% black on white; leave 1.75 x 1.0 in if you do not supply one; the template includes a barcode that may be moved but not resized; no price means add-on 90000.
- **Case laminate:** 0.625 in (16 mm) wrap on all sides, 0.5 in (13 mm) hinge each side of the spine; board = trim width - 0.185 in, trim height + 0.25 in.
- **Dust jacket:** 3.25 in flaps, 0.25 in wrap strip between cover and flap, 0.125 in bleed; panel = trim width + 0.4375 in, height + 0.25 in; trim size at most 6.14 x 9.252 in.
- **Print cost:** published formula "cost per page x number of pages + cost per unit". Current table: rate card effective 1 February 2026, with USD, GBP, AUD and EUR columns (39 product rows, all in facts.json).
- **Compensation:** list price less wholesale discount less print cost, and the Global Distribution Fee of 1.875% of the list price is also deducted.
- **Wholesale discount:** 40% to 55% for US and Canada; 35% to 55% for UK, EU and Australia.
- **Fees:** title setup free; no revision fee on the 2026 rate card.
- **Ebook:** EPUB (3 per the two August 2026 documents) plus a JPG front cover in RGB; 100 MB limit; compensation 85% of net revenue.
- **ISBN:** one per format; an Australian publisher brings its own (Thorpe-Bowker).
- **KDP:** allowed in parallel with your own ISBN, provided KDP Expanded Distribution is off.

## 4. Surprises and things to watch

1. **A 30% wholesale discount is below every published minimum.** Official pages say 40% (US, Canada) and 35% (UK, EU, Australia). The User Guide says the binding minimum is the one shown on the title setup page and that Ingram can change it. The public calculator does not validate the discount (it computed 10% and 60% without complaint), so it proves nothing. The account endpoint that returns the minimum redirects to login. Treat the minimum as an editable setting and expect 35% for AUD.
2. **The fee is now 1.875% of the list price**, not of the wholesale price. History found: free (October 2022 guide), 1% from July 2023, 1.5% on the April 2025 card, 1.875% on the February 2026 card. The calculator confirms the arithmetic: 30.00 AUD at 35% gives 19.50 - 7.46 print - 0.56 fee = 11.48.
3. **There IS a published per-unit and per-page price table**, including AUD. The live calculators reproduce it to the cent (44 comparisons across USD, GBP, EUR, AUD). So print cost does not have to be scraped from a calculator.
4. **Two rate cards are online.** `/print-discounts` still links the 2025 card and shows 2025 example prices. The User Guide link `/hubfs/downloads/pricing.pdf` serves the 2026 card (file last modified 17 November 2026) and the calculators use 2026 prices.
5. **Revisions appear to be free now.** The 2025 card charged 25 after 60 days. A letter of 10 December 2025 says "Free revisions are coming on January 1st" and the 2026 card has no revision line. The User Guide v3.3 still talks about revision charges and even "upload fees", so its wording is behind.
6. **No spine formula exists.** The paper sheet publishes PPI "minimums" (512 for 50lb white, 444 crème, 400 groundwood, 377 for 70lb white). The calculator returns thicker spines than pages / PPI, and for 50lb papers it is not even linear. Hardcover spines come back in 1/16 in steps with a 0.25 in minimum. Use the calculator or the template.
7. **The template generator cannot put an AUD price in the barcode.** Its currency list is US Dollars, UK Pounds, CA Dollars. For AUD the barcode gets the 90000 add-on unless you supply your own barcode.
8. **Australia-specific limits found:** no textured (linen) Digital Cloth cover in Australia; large-format groundwood paperback is US only; perforation is US only; the "Yes-Deliver" returns option is US market only. Groundwood itself is offered in Australia for most small trim sizes.
9. **There is no official list of what the Australian plant can print.** The Trim Size Matrix has no per-plant columns, and the Print and Ship Calculator priced a textured cloth hardcover at "LS AU" although the User Guide says it is not available there. Do not infer plant capability from the calculator.
10. **Digital Cloth spine stamping is Latin-only** (18 pt Cheltenham Bold, Latin letters, digits, basic punctuation). A Bengali title cannot be stamped.
11. **Ebook rules conflict across official sources** (EPUB 2 or 3 vs EPUB 3 only; 3.2 vs 5.6 million pixels per image; cover 1873 vs 2560 px). The strictest combination is recorded.
12. **Ebook compensation is "85% of net revenue"** in the User Guide and on the pricing page. The browser code of the tool contains an older-looking "40% of list" calculation; I followed the documents.
13. **The live tool offers more than the guide's matrix:** 41 standard trim sizes (the matrix has 32) and an Ultra Premium Color option that the matrix does not show. For the 32 shared sizes the two agree, except A5 case laminate on groundwood (shaded in the guide, absent in the tool).
14. **B&W 70lb white hardcover:** the tool allows 900 pages, the rate card and guide say 840.
15. **The last page.** Guide: last page will be blank for manufacturing information. Checklist: Ingram adds a barcode page (plus a blank page first if your PDF has an even number of pages). Title-setup blog: page count must be even and the last even page cannot have content.
16. **Maximum list price is 124.99** in USD, GBP and AUD alike. AUD compensation payments need an Australian bank and reach a 25.00 minimum.
17. **Canada is ambiguous.** Blogs mention CAD pricing and a "US and Canada" discount range; the public calculator has no Canada market and the rate card has no CAD column.
18. **GST.** The only official sentence found is in a 2022 blog: "AUD (Australia - GST Exclusive)". The calculator arithmetic agrees with a GST-exclusive list price. For a 1-copy quote to NSW the calculator showed tax on the handling fee and on shipping but none on the print charge.
19. **Misprints in the File Creation Guide:** ".4375 in (5mm)" for the dust-jacket panel (0.4375 in is about 11 mm), "0.0325 in" once for the narrow spine safety (0.03125 elsewhere), groundwood "38lb/48gsm" (56 gsm elsewhere).
20. **The trim-size matrix has no text in its cells.** Availability is shown by cell shading. I decoded the vector fill colours of page 40 and checked the result by eye against a rendering; it is marked as derived in facts.json.

### 4.1 Calculators: interactive only, but their data is readable

- The four tools (Publisher Comp. Calculator, Print and Ship Calculator, Weight and Spine Width Calculator, Cover Template Generator) are JavaScript apps. The pages contain no static tables.
- The JSON endpoints they call answered without login or cookies on 2026-11-23: trim size list, book types per trim size, page limits per book type, markets, print cost and compensation, spine width and weight, print locations, publisher-order quotes. Exact URLs and request bodies are in `facts.json` under `calculators_and_endpoints`.
- They are not a documented API. The JavaScript file names contain build hashes and the paths may change. An app should treat them as optional and fall back to the published rate card.
- Endpoints that DO need login: the account's minimum discount, the title-setup lookup data (language list), the trim-size matrix model.
- Requests made for this research: 42 trim-size lists, 984 book types, 149 spine calculations, 15 compensation calculations, 2 print-and-ship quotes, a few single probes; all throttled.


## 5. Contradictions between official sources

Each item is in `facts.json` under `contradictions_between_official_sources` with the verbatim quotes. Short list:

1. **Maximum upload file size.** Use 1.5 GB.
2. **EPUB version.** Produce EPUB 3.
3. **Largest image inside an EPUB.** Stay at or below 3.2 million pixels to satisfy both documents dated 24 Aug 2026.
4. **Ebook cover pixel size.** A cover at least 1600 px wide and 2560 px tall meets every statement.
5. **EPUB validator recommended.** Validate with a current EPUB validator; Ingram does not name a specific EPUBCheck version.
6. **Ebook compensation.** Documents say 85% of net revenue; the 40%-of-list calculation exists only in tool code. Use the documents.
7. **Revision fee.** The 2026 rate card lists no revision fee and no setup fee; treat revisions as free but let the user confirm at checkout. The User Guide v3.3 still contains older wording about upload fees and revision charges.
8. **Which rate card is current.** The /print-discounts page still links the 2025 card and shows 2025 example prices; the User Guide link (/hubfs/downloads/pricing.pdf) and the live calculators use the 2026 card. Use 2026.
9. **Global distribution (market access) fee percentage over time.** Not a contradiction but a history: 1% (July 2023), 1.5% (April 2025 card), 1.875% (February 2026 card). Keep the percentage editable.
10. **Compensation formula omits the fee in the User Guide.** Deduct the fee: the live calculator does (list x (1 - discount) - print - 1.875% of list).
11. **Minimum wholesale discount.** Published minimums: 40% US/Canada, 35% UK/EU/AU. A 30% setting is below all of them; verify in the account.
12. **Groundwood paper weight in gsm.** Three sources say 56 gsm; the guide matrix header says 48 gsm. Cosmetic.
13. **Groundwood pages per inch.** Do not compute spine from PPI; use the calculator.
14. **Crème paper weight in gsm.** Cosmetic.
15. **Custom trim: is groundwood grouped with white or with crème for perfect bound.** Tool data allows custom groundwood perfect bound up to 8.5 x 11, but the rate card marks large groundwood paperbacks "US and short run ONLY". For an Australian publisher treat groundwood as small-format only (width under 6.15 in).
16. **Custom trim maximum length in mm.** Use inches (11 in).
17. **Smallest hardcover.** Smallest standard hardcover is 5 x 8; custom hardcover can go down to 5 x 7 per the guide and the tool.
18. **B&W 70lb white hardcover maximum pages.** Use 840.
19. **Dust jacket panel offset in mm.** 0.4375 in is about 11 mm; the "(5mm)" in the guide is a misprint. Use inches.
20. **Dust jacket flap in mm.** Use 3.25 in.
21. **Cover line screen.** Informational only.
22. **Last page of the interior.** End the PDF on an odd page (Ingram adds the barcode page) or on an even blank page; never put content on a final even page.
23. **Spine safety threshold wording.** Treat exactly 0.35 in as "wide" (0.0625 in); the 0.0325 figure on the duplex page is a misprint of 0.03125.
24. **Duplex cover surcharge outside the US.** The second quote is the calculator response for a duplex cover on book type 23 (6 x 9 B&W 50lb white, 200 pages): print 4.55/3.94/4.33/7.86 versus 4.25/3.69/4.33/7.46 for a normal cover, i.e. +0.30 USD, +0.25 GBP, +0.00 EUR, +0.40 AUD instead of the rate-card values. Low impact; keep the rate-card numbers and flag.
25. **Number of retailers in the network.** Marketing figure; ignore.
26. **RTL language examples.** Not relevant to Bengali (left-to-right).

## 6. What could NOT be verified

Nothing below was filled in from memory or from third-party sites; in `facts.json` these carry `"verified": false` and a note.

1. **Help-centre articles (help.ingramspark.com).** Every request returned HTTP 403 with a Cloudflare bot challenge (curl and WebFetch); the Zendesk API returned 401. Not bypassed. Topics that live only there were not read: detailed EPUB guidelines, Australian GST/tax articles, the Amazon/KDP article, book-building tool details, market list, Global Connect printer list.
2. **Whether a 30% wholesale discount is accepted in any market.** Official pages say minimum 40% (US, Canada) and 35% (UK, EU, AU). The enforced minimum is shown only on the title setup page (login); the endpoint /Titles/MarketPricing/CustomerMarketDiscountMin redirects to login.
3. **Wholesale discount range for Global Connect.** Only stated that Global Connect uses the US price and discount.
4. **Canada as a pricing market (CAD list price) today.** Mentioned in a 2022 blog and a blog updated Nov 2025; absent from the public calculator market list and the rate card.
5. **Whether the AUD list price is GST-inclusive or exclusive, from a current document.** Only a May 2022 blog says "AUD (Australia - GST Exclusive)"; the calculator arithmetic is consistent with GST-exclusive. No 2025-2026 document addresses it.
6. **Official spine-width formula.** None published. PPI values are published (paper spec sheet, "minimums"); the calculator is non-linear for 50lb papers and gives larger values than pages/PPI.
7. **Exact values printed on a generated cover template.** Generator not submitted (needs ISBN and email).
8. **Per-trim-size list of what Lightning Source Australia can print.** No document has per-plant columns. Only: no textured Digital Cloth in AU; no large groundwood paperback (US only); no perforation (US only); groundwood available in AU for most small trim sizes. 12. **EPUBCheck by name/version.** Ingram names the IDPF validator site and the pagina EPUB-Checker; no EPUBCheck version is specified.
13. **Which ebook rule is right where documents disagree (EPUB 2 vs 3; 3.2M vs 5.6M pixels; cover 1873 vs 2560 px).** All four sources are official; none says which supersedes the other.
14. **Revision fee today.** Inferred as free: December 2025 letter announces free revisions from 1 January 2026 and the 2026 rate card lists none; no explicit post-January confirmation page was found and the User Guide still mentions revision charges.
15. **Exact dates on which the global distribution fee changed.** Only rate-card effective dates (April 1st, 2025; February 1st, 2026) and "in July" 2023.
16. **Bengali (Bangla) support for print metadata language, ebook retailers, book-building tool.** Not mentioned on any reachable official page; language drop-down is behind login.
17. **AUD price add-on in the barcode.** The template generator offers only USD, UKP and CAD for "Price in Bar Code"; no official statement about AUD add-ons.

20. **Tax (GST) treatment of publisher-direct orders in Australia.** Only calculator behaviour observed (tax on handling and shipping lines); no published rule read.

## 8. Suggestions for the app's spec file

- Keep these editable, because Ingram changes them: Global Distribution Fee percentage (changed three times since 2023), the rate table (annual change, capped at 5%), minimum wholesale discount per market, revision fee, maximum list price.
- Store print prices as the rate-card rows (size class, binding, interior, paper, per-cover, per-page, per currency). Size class is "small" when trim width is under 6.15 in.
- Do not compute spine width from PPI. Either call the spine calculator endpoint for the chosen book type and page count, or ask the user to paste the spine width printed on the cover template.
- For Australia: do not offer textured Digital Cloth, perforation or large-format groundwood; default the barcode to no price (90000) because the generator has no AUD option; warn when the wholesale discount is under 35%.
- For Bengali: require embedded fonts in the PDF; put the English title first in the metadata title (400-byte limit, Bengali characters take 3 bytes each in UTF-8, my arithmetic); do not offer stamped spine text on Digital Cloth. Bengali itself is not mentioned on any official page I could read.
- For ebooks use the strictest reading until Ingram reconciles its documents: EPUB 3, images of at most 3.2 million pixels, cover JPG in RGB at least 1600 px wide and 2560 px tall, files under 100 MB, no page-number references.
