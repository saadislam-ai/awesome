# Translation Agreement

> DRAFT for review by an Australian lawyer before first use. This is not legal advice. Use it when you commission a translation (for example from Bangla into English, or English into Bangla). First make sure you hold the translation rights from the author of the original work: for your own books you do; for anyone else's, the author publishing agreement must grant them.

## In plain words

- The Translator translates the book for an agreed fee, plus a share of what the translation earns.
- The Translator is named as translator on the cover and title page.
- The Translator owns the copyright in the translation and gives the Publisher an exclusive licence to publish it.
- Payment is in Australian dollars to the Translator's account; any transfer fees are paid by the Publisher.

## Parties

**Publisher:** [Full legal name] trading as Borderless Publishing, ABN [__________], of [address], New South Wales, Australia ("**Publisher**").

**Translator:** [Full legal name] of [address, country] ("**Translator**").

**Date:** [__________]

## 1. The work

1.1 The Translator will translate the work described in the Schedule (the "**Original Work**") from [Bangla] into [English] (the "**Translation**").

1.2 The Translation must be complete and faithful to the Original Work, in good idiomatic [Australian English / standard Bangla], without omissions or additions other than those agreed in writing with the Publisher.

## 2. Delivery

2.1 The Translator will deliver the complete Translation as an editable electronic file by the date in the Schedule. The parties may agree a sample of about [3,000] words first.

2.2 The Publisher will tell the Translator within [45] days of delivery whether the Translation is accepted or what reasonable revisions are needed. The Translator will make those revisions within [30] days.

2.3 The Translator will answer the editor's queries and check the edited text and proofs within [21] days of receiving them.

## 3. Fee and share of earnings

3.1 The Publisher will pay the Translator a fee of $[____] (being $[____] per 1,000 words of the Original Work, or a fixed sum), payable [half on signing and half on acceptance of the Translation].

3.2 In addition, the Publisher will pay the Translator [10] percent of Net Receipts from all editions of the Translation, as defined in the author publishing agreement for the Original Work, with statements and payment at the same times as the author's royalties. [Or: the fee is in full payment and no further share is paid.]

3.3 If the Translator is registered for GST, the Translator will issue a tax invoice and GST is paid on top. If the Translator lives outside Australia, the Publisher will check before the first payment whether Australian withholding tax applies, and will tell the Translator.

## 4. Rights

4.1 The Translator owns the copyright in the Translation, subject to the copyright of the author of the Original Work.

4.2 The Translator grants the Publisher the exclusive licence, worldwide and for the same period as the Publisher's rights in the Original Work, to reproduce, publish, distribute and sell the Translation in all formats the Publisher is entitled to publish the Original Work in.

4.3 The Publisher will not use the Translation to train an artificial intelligence system, or license it for that purpose, without the Translator's written consent.

## 5. Credit and moral rights

5.1 The Translator will be credited as "Translated from the [Bangla] by [name]" on the cover, the title page, and in the metadata of every edition.

5.2 The Translator consents to copyediting for accuracy, consistency and house style. Other changes to the Translation need the Translator's approval, which will not be unreasonably withheld.

## 6. Translator's promises

6.1 The Translator promises that the Translation is the Translator's own work (except for any software assistance disclosed in writing, which the Translator has checked line by line), does not copy any other translation, and contains nothing added that is defamatory or unlawful.

6.2 The Translator will not translate, or authorise anyone else to publish, a competing translation of the Original Work during the term of this agreement.

## 7. Ending the agreement

7.1 If the Translator does not deliver an acceptable Translation within [90] days after the delivery date, the Publisher may end this agreement by written notice. The first instalment of the fee is not repayable, and the Translator's rights in the work delivered return to the Translator.

7.2 If the Publisher has not published the Translation within [18] months of acceptance, the Translator keeps all fees paid, and either party may end this agreement by written notice.

## 8. General

8.1 This agreement is governed by the law of New South Wales, Australia. The parties will try to resolve any dispute by discussion and then mediation before going to court.

8.2 Notices may be given by email to the addresses in the Schedule. This agreement may be signed electronically and in counterparts.
