# Freelance Services Agreement

> DRAFT for review by an Australian lawyer before first use. This is not legal advice. One agreement for editors, proofreaders, cover designers, illustrators and typesetters. Fill in one Schedule for each job. For cover art, choose in item 8 of the Schedule whether the artwork's copyright is transferred to you (assignment) or licensed to you; a transfer must be in writing and signed by the artist to be valid.

## In plain words

- The Freelancer does a defined job for a fixed fee, by a date.
- The Publisher pays within [14] days of an invoice for each stage.
- For artwork, the Publisher gets the rights it needs for every edition and for marketing; the Freelancer keeps the right to show the work in a portfolio and is credited.
- Each side keeps the other's information confidential.

## 1. The services

1.1 The Freelancer will provide the services described in the Schedule (the "**Services**") with reasonable care and skill, and to the standard expected of a professional in the field.

1.2 The Freelancer is an independent contractor and not an employee. The Freelancer is responsible for the Freelancer's own tax, superannuation and insurance.

1.3 Changes to the Services must be agreed in writing (email is enough), including any change to the fee or the dates.

## 2. Materials and approvals

2.1 The Publisher will supply the manuscript, brief, specifications and other materials listed in the Schedule.

2.2 The Publisher will give feedback on each stage within the time in the Schedule. The fee includes the number of revision rounds stated in the Schedule; further rounds are charged at the rate in the Schedule.

## 3. Fee and payment

3.1 The Publisher will pay the fee in the Schedule. The Freelancer will invoice at each payment stage in the Schedule, and the Publisher will pay within [14] days of receiving a correct invoice.

3.2 If the Freelancer is registered for GST, the fee is exclusive of GST and the invoice must be a tax invoice.

3.3 If the Publisher cancels the Services, it will pay for the work done up to the cancellation, and at least the stage payment that has fallen due.

## 4. Rights in the work

4.1 **Editing, proofreading and typesetting.** The Freelancer's corrections and changes to the Publisher's text belong to the copyright owner of the text. The Freelancer has no claim to them.

4.2 **Artwork (cover design and illustration).** As chosen in item 8 of the Schedule:

(a) **Assignment:** on payment of the fee in full, the Freelancer assigns to the Publisher all copyright in the final artwork created under this agreement, throughout the world; or

(b) **Licence:** on payment of the fee in full, the Freelancer grants the Publisher an exclusive, worldwide, irrevocable licence to use the final artwork for the book named in the Schedule, in every edition and format of that book (including later editions, translations, audiobooks and large print) and in advertising, publicity and merchandise for that book, for the full period of copyright. The Freelancer may not license the final artwork to anyone else.

4.3 In both cases the Freelancer may show the artwork in a portfolio and on the Freelancer's own website and social media, with credit to the book, after the book's cover has been made public by the Publisher.

4.4 Unused sketches and concepts remain the Freelancer's property, but the Freelancer will not use them for another book cover in the same genre without the Publisher's agreement.

4.5 The Freelancer promises that the work is original, does not infringe anyone's rights, and that any stock photographs, fonts or other materials used are properly licensed for book covers, print runs without limit, ebooks and marketing. The Freelancer will give the Publisher copies of those licences. Any use of image generating software must be disclosed in writing beforehand and needs the Publisher's agreement, because publishing platforms ask about it.

## 5. Credit and moral rights

5.1 The Freelancer will be credited as stated in the Schedule (for example "Cover design by [name]" on the copyright page).

5.2 The Freelancer consents to the Publisher resizing and cropping the artwork, adding the title, author name, barcode and logo, and adapting it to the dimensions of each edition, and to the use of the artwork without credit where credit is not practical (for example in a store listing thumbnail). This consent is limited to those acts.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Freelancer will keep the manuscript and all unpublished information about the book confidential, will not share it or upload it to any public service or to any artificial intelligence tool that may keep or learn from it, and will delete or return it when the Services are complete, except for one archive copy of the Freelancer's own work.

## 7. Ending the agreement

7.1 Either party may end this agreement by written notice if the other does not fix a material breach within 14 days of written notice describing it. The Publisher then pays for work properly done up to the end. Rights under clause 4 pass only for work that has been paid for.

## 8. General

8.1 Each party's liability to the other is limited to the total fee, except for breach of clause 4.5 or 6, or for deliberate wrongdoing.

8.2 This agreement is governed by the law of New South Wales, Australia. It may be signed electronically, and an exchange of emails accepting the Schedule is enough to form the agreement.
