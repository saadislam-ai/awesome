# Letter of Arrangement with Blessings Foundation Bangladesh

> DRAFT for review by a lawyer and your accountant before first use. This is not legal advice. Your promise that "any margin is donated" is a public representation, so it must be true, clear and verifiable (Australian Consumer Law), and in some states selling goods with a statement that proceeds go to a charity can be a regulated fundraising activity. Settle the points in the setup checklist (03-business) before you print such a statement on a book or a website. This letter records the arrangement with the charity so both sides know what to expect.

Dear [Name],

**Donations from Borderless Publishing**

I am writing to set out how Borderless Publishing will donate to Blessings Foundation Bangladesh ("**the Foundation**"), so that we have a shared record of it.

**1. What Borderless Publishing does.** Borderless Publishing is a small independent publisher in Sydney, Australia, run by [full legal name] as a sole trader [or: company]. It publishes books at prices set as close as practical to the lowest price each bookseller allows, so that they reach as many readers as possible.

**2. What will be donated.** After paying the direct costs of publishing (such as editing, covers, ISBNs, proof copies and distribution fees) and the share paid to each author, Borderless Publishing will donate [all / ____ percent] of its remaining margin from book sales to the Foundation. This is a voluntary donation decided by Borderless Publishing each year; it is not a fee for a service and the Foundation provides nothing in return.

**3. When.** Donations will be made [once a year, by 31 March, for the year ending 31 December before it / every six months].

**4. How.** By bank transfer in Australian dollars to the account the Foundation nominates in writing. Borderless Publishing will pay its own bank's transfer fees.

**5. Receipts and statements.** The Foundation will send a written receipt for each donation, showing the amount received and the date. Borderless Publishing will publish a yearly statement of the total donated, and may say that the Foundation confirmed receipt.

**6. Use of the name.** The Foundation agrees that Borderless Publishing may name the Foundation, and describe its work in the words below, on its website, in its books and in publicity, for as long as this arrangement continues:

"[Short description of the Foundation's work, approved by the Foundation.]"

Borderless Publishing will not use the Foundation's logo, or suggest that the Foundation endorses any book, without the Foundation's written approval.

**7. Changes and ending.** Either of us may end this arrangement by written notice. Donations already due for a period before the notice will still be made. If it ends, Borderless Publishing will stop naming the Foundation as the recipient of its donations within 60 days.

**8. Information we need from you.** Please confirm: (a) the Foundation's full legal name and registration number in Bangladesh; (b) the bank account for donations; (c) the person we should contact; and (d) the description in item 6.

If this matches your understanding, please sign below and return a copy.

Yours sincerely,

[Name]
Borderless Publishing
