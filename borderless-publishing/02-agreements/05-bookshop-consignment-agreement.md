# Bookshop Consignment Agreement

> DRAFT for review before first use. This is not legal advice. Many independent bookshops will stock a local publisher's books "on consignment": the shop pays only for copies it sells. Most bookshops that order through a wholesaler can also order your books from IngramSpark; consignment is for shops that prefer to deal with you directly, and for events.

## Parties

**Publisher:** [Full legal name] trading as Borderless Publishing, ABN [__________], of [address] ("**Publisher**").

**Bookshop:** [Business name], ABN [__________], of [address] ("**Bookshop**").

**Date:** [__________]

## 1. Stock

1.1 The Publisher supplies the books listed on each delivery note to the Bookshop on consignment. The books remain the Publisher's property until the Bookshop has sold them and paid for them.

1.2 Each delivery note lists the titles, ISBNs, quantities, recommended retail prices and the date. Both parties sign or email to confirm it.

## 2. Price and the Bookshop's share

2.1 The Bookshop will sell the books at the recommended retail price on the delivery note, or another price agreed in writing.

2.2 The Bookshop keeps [35] percent of the retail price of each copy sold and pays the Publisher the remaining [65] percent. [Note: because Borderless prices are close to printing cost, check that the remaining amount still covers your cost of the copies before agreeing a share.]

2.3 If the Publisher is registered for GST, the amounts include GST and the Publisher will issue tax invoices; if not, no GST is charged.

## 3. Sales reports and payment

3.1 At the end of each [month / quarter], the Bookshop will send the Publisher a report of copies sold and copies in stock, and will pay for the copies sold within [30] days of the end of the period.

3.2 The Publisher may count the stock at a time agreed with the Bookshop.

## 4. Display, care and loss

4.1 The Bookshop will display the books in a reasonable way and keep them in saleable condition.

4.2 The Bookshop is responsible for copies lost, stolen or damaged while in its care, and will pay for them as if sold.

## 5. Returns and ending

5.1 Either party may end the arrangement for a title, or altogether, by [14] days' written notice. The Bookshop will then return unsold copies in saleable condition, or make them available for collection, and pay for all copies sold.

5.2 The Publisher may ask for the return of copies at any time to refresh stock or deal with corrections.

## 6. General

6.1 This agreement is governed by the law of New South Wales, Australia.
