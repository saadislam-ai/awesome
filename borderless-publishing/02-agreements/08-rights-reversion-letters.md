# Rights Reversion Letters

> Two letters. Letter A is what an author sends to ask for their rights back under clause 15 of the Author Publishing Agreement. Letter B is your confirmation once you have withdrawn the book. Give authors Letter A along with their contract, so they know how it works.

## Letter A: request from the Author

[Date]

Borderless Publishing
[Address or email]

Dear Publisher,

**Request for reversion of rights in "[Title]"**

I am the author of "[Title]", published by you under our Author Publishing Agreement dated [date].

Under clause [15.1 / 15.2 / 3.2] of that agreement, I ask that all rights granted to you return to me. [The reason: for example, fewer than 25 copies were sold in the two accounting periods ending [dates] / the agreement has reached the end of its term and I give 90 days' notice.]

Please withdraw the book from sale on every platform, send me a final royalty statement, and confirm the date on which my rights return to me.

Yours sincerely,

[Author's name]
