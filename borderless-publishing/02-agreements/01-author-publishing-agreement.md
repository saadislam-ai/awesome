# Author Publishing Agreement

> DRAFT for review by an Australian lawyer before first use. This is not legal advice. Text in square brackets is to be filled in or chosen. Notes like this one are for the publisher and are removed before the agreement is sent.

## In plain words

This page is a summary for the Author. The clauses that follow are the agreement; where this summary and the clauses differ, the clauses apply.

- **You keep your copyright.** You give Borderless Publishing a licence to publish your book in the formats ticked in Schedule 1, for a fixed term.
- **You pay nothing.** We pay for editing, cover, typesetting, ISBNs, proofs and distribution. We will never ask you for money to publish your book.
- **Prices are low on purpose.** We set each price close to the lowest the platforms allow, so the book reaches as many readers as possible. That means the money per copy is small.
- **You receive [50] percent of what we receive** for each copy, after the platform's share and the printing cost. You receive a statement every six months.
- **Our share goes to charity.** After our direct costs, our share of any margin is donated to Blessings Foundation Bangladesh, and we publish a yearly statement of what was donated. You may choose to donate your share too, but you do not have to.
- **You approve the text and are consulted on the cover.** Nothing is changed in your words without your approval.
- **You can get your rights back**: at the end of the term, if the book stops selling, or if we do not publish on time.

## Background

A. The Author is the author and owner of copyright in the work described in Schedule 1.

B. The Publisher publishes books at prices set close to cost so that they reach as many readers as possible, and donates its share of any margin to charity as described in its Donation Policy.

C. The parties agree that the Publisher will publish the work on the terms of this agreement.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this agreement:

**Work** means the work described in Schedule 1, including its title, text, any illustrations and other material supplied by the Author, and any revised edition.

**Formats** means the formats ticked in Schedule 1 (for example paperback, hardcover, large print, ebook, audiobook).

**Territory** means the territory stated in Schedule 1.

**Term** means the period stated in clause 3.

**Platform** means a printer, distributor, retailer or aggregator through which the Publisher sells or distributes the Work, such as Amazon KDP, IngramSpark and Draft2Digital.

**Net Receipts** means the money the Publisher actually receives from the sale or licensing of the Work in a Format, after the Platform has taken its share, and less only: (a) the printing and delivery cost the Platform charges for printed copies; (b) any GST or other sales tax included in the amount received; and (c) refunds and returns. No other costs are deducted.

**Charity** means Blessings Foundation Bangladesh, or another charity named by the Publisher under its Donation Policy.

**Donation Policy** means the Publisher's published policy on donations, as updated from time to time, a copy of which has been given to the Author.

**Publication Date** means the date on which the Work is first made available for sale in any Format.

## 2. Grant of rights

2.1 The Author grants the Publisher the exclusive licence, during the Term and in the Territory, to reproduce, publish, distribute and sell the Work in the Formats, in the language stated in Schedule 1.

2.2 The Author keeps the copyright in the Work and all rights not expressly granted in this agreement, including translation rights, film, television, stage and radio rights, and merchandising rights, unless Schedule 1 says otherwise.

2.3 The Publisher may use the Author's name, approved biography and approved photograph, and extracts of the Work of up to [10] percent, to publicise and sell the Work during the Term.

2.4 The Publisher will not use the Work, or allow it to be used, to train, test or improve an artificial intelligence system, and will not license it for that purpose, without the Author's separate written consent.

## 3. Term and renewal

3.1 This agreement starts on the date above and continues for [five] years from the Publication Date (the "**Initial Term**").

3.2 After the Initial Term, it continues from year to year until either party ends it by giving the other at least 90 days' written notice.

3.3 If the Work has not been published in at least one Format within [12] months after the Publisher accepts the final manuscript, the Author may end this agreement by written notice, and all rights return to the Author.

## 4. Delivery and acceptance

4.1 The Author will deliver the complete manuscript by the date in Schedule 1, as an editable electronic file, together with any illustrations, permissions and other material listed in Schedule 1.

4.2 The Publisher will tell the Author within [60] days of delivery whether the manuscript is accepted, or what revisions are needed for it to be accepted. Acceptance will not be unreasonably withheld.

4.3 If after one round of reasonable revisions the parties cannot agree that the manuscript is ready to publish, either party may end this agreement by written notice. Neither party then owes the other anything.

## 5. Publishing at no cost to the Author

5.1 The Publisher pays all costs of publishing the Work, including editing, proofreading, cover design, typesetting, ISBNs, proof copies, distribution set up and legal deposit.

5.2 The Author is not required to pay any fee, to buy any copies, or to pay for any service as a condition of publication.

## 6. Editing, approval and design

6.1 The Publisher may copyedit the Work for consistency, accuracy and house style (including spelling, punctuation and the presentation of dashes and quotation marks). The Publisher will not otherwise change the text, title or meaning of the Work without the Author's approval, which will not be unreasonably withheld or delayed.

6.2 The Author will receive the edited text and final proofs, and will approve or return corrections within [21] days. Corrections other than printer's errors are limited to those reasonably needed.

6.3 The Publisher decides the design, cover, format and price of the Work after consulting the Author in good faith. The Author's reasonable objections to the cover will be taken into account.

6.4 The Author will be credited on the cover, spine and title page of every printed edition, and in the metadata of every edition, in the form stated in Schedule 1.

## 7. Moral rights

7.1 The Publisher will respect the Author's moral rights under the Copyright Act 1968 (Cth), including the right to be attributed as author and the right of integrity.

7.2 The Author consents to the following acts which might otherwise infringe the Author's moral rights: copyediting under clause 6.1; abridgement of extracts for publicity; and changes of layout, typeface and format needed to produce each Format. This consent is limited to those acts.

## 8. Publication and pricing

8.1 The Publisher will publish the Work in at least the first Format listed in Schedule 1 within [12] months of accepting the final manuscript, and will keep it available for sale during the Term.

8.2 The Publisher sets prices under its Reach First Pricing Policy: as close as practical to the lowest price each Platform allows, plus a small margin. The Author acknowledges that royalties per copy will therefore be lower than at usual market prices.

8.3 The Publisher will tell the Author the price of each Format before publication and when it changes. The Author may ask the Publisher to review a price, and the Publisher will consider the request in good faith.

8.4 The Publisher will obtain ISBNs for each Format in the Publisher's name, and will make the legal deposits required by law.

## 9. Royalties

9.1 The Publisher will pay the Author [50] percent of Net Receipts from every Format, and [50] percent of Net Receipts from any licence of the Work that the Author has agreed to.

9.2 No advance is paid. [Or: The Publisher will pay the Author an advance of $[____] on signing, which is recouped from royalties and is not repayable.]

9.3 No royalty is payable on copies given free to the Author, to reviewers, to libraries for legal deposit, or for publicity, or on copies destroyed or returned.

9.4 **Author's donation option.** The Author may by written notice at any time ask the Publisher to pay some or all of the Author's royalties directly to the Charity. The Publisher will do so and will show the payment on the Author's statement. The Author is responsible for any tax consequences of that choice and is encouraged to take advice.

9.5 The Publisher's own share of Net Receipts, after the Publisher's direct costs of publishing the Work, is applied under the Donation Policy. This clause is a statement of the Publisher's policy and does not give the Author or the Charity any right to enforce it.

## 10. Statements and payment

10.1 The Publisher will send the Author a royalty statement for each six month period ending 30 June and 31 December, within [60] days after the end of the period. The statement will show, for each Format and Platform, copies sold, Net Receipts and royalties.

10.2 Royalties are paid with each statement into the Author's nominated bank account, in Australian dollars. If the amount due is less than $[20], it is carried forward to the next period, but everything owing is paid at least once a year.

10.3 If the Author is registered for GST, the Author will give the Publisher a tax invoice, and the Publisher will pay GST on top of the royalty. [Note: if the Author is overseas, ask your accountant whether withholding tax applies before the first payment.]

10.4 The Author may, at the Author's cost and once a year, have an accountant inspect the Publisher's records relating to the Work on 14 days' notice. If an underpayment of more than 5 percent is found, the Publisher will pay the shortfall and the reasonable cost of the inspection.

## 11. Author copies

11.1 The Publisher will give the Author [10] free copies of each printed Format on publication, and the ebook file.

11.2 The Author may buy further copies at the Publisher's printing and delivery cost, for any purpose including resale at events.

## 12. Author's promises

12.1 The Author promises that:

(a) the Author is the sole author of the Work, or has disclosed any co-author in Schedule 1, and has the right to enter this agreement;

(b) the Work is original, has not been published in the Territory in the Formats before (except as disclosed in Schedule 1), and does not infringe anyone's copyright or other rights;

(c) the Work contains nothing defamatory, nothing that breaches confidence or privacy, and nothing unlawful, and that statements presented as fact are true or honestly believed after reasonable care;

(d) any recipe, formula, instruction or advice in the Work will not, if followed, cause injury or damage, and appropriate warnings are included;

(e) the Author has disclosed in writing any use of artificial intelligence tools in creating the text or images of the Work, so that the Publisher can answer the questions the Platforms ask about such content truthfully; and

(f) the Author has obtained, or will obtain before delivery, written permission for any material owned by others that appears in the Work, at the Author's cost unless Schedule 1 says otherwise.

12.2 The Author will tell the Publisher straight away if the Author learns that any of these promises is untrue.

12.3 If a claim is made that the Work breaches clause 12.1, the Publisher may delay or stop publication or withdraw the Work, and the parties will cooperate in good faith to deal with the claim.

12.4 The Author will compensate the Publisher for loss reasonably incurred as a direct result of a breach of clause 12.1, except to the extent the loss was caused by the Publisher's changes to the Work. The Author's liability under this clause is limited to [the royalties received under this agreement] [$_____], except where the breach was deliberate.

> Note for the publisher: indemnity and its limit are a common point of negotiation. For memoir and nonfiction that names real people, consider a legal read before publication and publisher's liability insurance.

## 13. Publisher's promises

13.1 The Publisher promises to:

(a) publish the Work to a professional standard in line with the Formats and this agreement;

(b) credit the Author correctly and respect the Author's moral rights;

(c) keep accurate records of sales and Net Receipts for at least five years;

(d) keep the Author's personal information private and use it only for this agreement; and

(e) apply its share of Net Receipts under the Donation Policy and publish a yearly donation statement.

## 14. Revised editions

14.1 If the Publisher reasonably considers a revised edition necessary, the Author will be offered the first opportunity to revise the Work on agreed terms. Revised editions are covered by this agreement.

## 15. Reversion of rights

15.1 The Author may by written notice ask for the return of rights if, in any two consecutive accounting periods after the first two years following the Publication Date, fewer than [25] copies in all Formats combined have been sold. Rights return to the Author 60 days after the notice.

15.2 The Author may end this agreement by written notice if the Publisher: (a) does not send a statement or pay royalties within 60 days after a written reminder; (b) stops having the Work available for sale in every Format for more than six months; or (c) becomes bankrupt, insolvent or stops trading.

15.3 When rights return to the Author, the Publisher will within 60 days withdraw the Work from sale on every Platform, tell the Author when it has done so, and send a final statement. The Publisher may sell copies it already holds for a further six months. The Author may buy the Publisher's print files and cover files at no cost, but may not use the Publisher's logo or ISBNs, which belong to the Publisher.

## 16. Ending the agreement

16.1 Either party may end this agreement by written notice if the other materially breaches it and does not fix the breach within 30 days of written notice describing it.

16.2 Ending the agreement does not affect royalties already earned, clauses 10.4, 12 and 17, or any licence already granted to a third party with the Author's agreement.

## 17. General

17.1 **Notices** must be in writing and may be sent by email to the addresses in Schedule 1.

17.2 **Disputes.** The parties will first try in good faith to resolve any dispute by discussion, and then by mediation through the Arts Law Centre of Australia or another mediator they agree on, before starting court proceedings (except for urgent relief).

17.3 **Governing law.** This agreement is governed by the law of New South Wales, Australia, and the parties submit to the courts of New South Wales.

17.4 **Entire agreement.** This agreement, with its Schedules and the Donation Policy referred to in it, is the whole agreement between the parties about the Work. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.

17.5 **Assignment.** The Publisher may not transfer this agreement without the Author's written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld if the Publisher's business is transferred as a whole to a publisher that agrees to be bound by it.

17.6 **Counterparts and electronic signature.** This agreement may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature.
