# Permission Request and Grant

> Use this when a book quotes material that belongs to someone else: an epigraph, song lyrics, a poem, a long passage, a photograph, a map or an illustration. Short quotations for criticism or review may be allowed without permission (fair dealing under the Copyright Act 1968), but song lyrics, poems and epigraphs usually need permission. Send one letter for each rights holder; keep the signed grant with the book's files.
