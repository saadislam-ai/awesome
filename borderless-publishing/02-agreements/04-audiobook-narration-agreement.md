# Audiobook Narration and Production Agreement

> DRAFT for review by an Australian lawyer before first use. This is not legal advice. Use it when a narrator records, and usually also edits and masters, an audiobook for you. Borderless Studio's Audiobook tab produces the script files, the pronunciation guide and the requirements list referred to below. Check that your agreement with the author grants audiobook rights before you sign this.

## In plain words

- The Narrator records the book from the scripts we supply and delivers files that meet the audio requirements attached.
- Payment is either a fee per finished hour, or a share of audiobook earnings, or a mix (choose in the Schedule).
- The Narrator is credited as narrator; the Publisher owns the recording.
- The Narrator's voice will not be used to train or make a synthetic voice.

## 1. The recording

1.1 The Narrator will record an unabridged reading of the book named in the Schedule (the "**Book**") from the script files supplied by the Publisher, using the pronunciation guide supplied, and will deliver the recording as separate files for each section, meeting the audio requirements in the attached requirements list (the "**Recording**").

1.2 The Narrator will deliver a recording of the first 15 minutes for approval before recording the rest. The Publisher will approve it or give notes within [7] days.

1.3 The Publisher (and the author, if the Publisher chooses) will listen to the Recording and send a list of corrections within [21] days. The Narrator will correct reading errors, mispronunciations and technical faults at no extra charge. Changes the Publisher makes to the text after recording are charged at the pickup rate in the Schedule.

## 2. Payment

2.1 The Publisher will pay the Narrator as chosen in the Schedule:

(a) **Fee:** $[____] per finished hour of the final Recording, paid within [14] days of approval of the final Recording; or

(b) **Share of earnings:** [25] percent of the Publisher's Net Receipts from the audiobook (as defined in the author publishing agreement for the Book), for the period the Publisher distributes the Recording, with statements twice a year; or

(c) **Mix:** a fee of $[____] per finished hour plus [10] percent of Net Receipts.

2.2 If the Narrator is registered for GST, GST is added to the fee and the Narrator will issue a tax invoice.

## 3. Rights

3.1 The Publisher owns the copyright in the Recording (the sound recording), and on payment the Narrator assigns to the Publisher any copyright the Narrator may have in the Recording.

3.2 The Publisher may sell and distribute the Recording in every audio format and through any platform, and may use extracts of up to five minutes for promotion.

3.3 The Publisher will not, and will not allow anyone to, use the Recording or the Narrator's voice to create, train or improve a synthetic voice or any artificial intelligence system, without the Narrator's separate written consent.

## 4. Credit and performer's rights

4.1 The Narrator will be credited as "Narrated by [name]" in the audiobook's metadata and in the opening and closing credits.

4.2 The Narrator consents to the Recording being edited for length of pauses, removal of noises and joining of takes, and to the opening and closing credits being added. This consent is limited to those acts.

## 5. Narrator's promises

5.1 The Narrator promises that the Recording will be the Narrator's own performance, recorded in a suitable quiet space, and that no part of it will be generated by a synthetic voice.

5.2 The Narrator will keep the script confidential until the audiobook is published.

## 6. Ending the agreement

6.1 If the Narrator cannot complete the Recording, the Publisher pays for approved finished hours delivered, and owns them on payment. If the Publisher cancels the Recording, it pays for finished hours delivered and for the hours recorded to that date at the rate in the Schedule.

## 7. General

7.1 This agreement is governed by the law of New South Wales, Australia. It may be signed electronically and in counterparts.
