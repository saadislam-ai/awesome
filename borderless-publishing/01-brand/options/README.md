# Five brand options

Five directions for the Borderless Publishing brand. Open `borderless-brand-options.pdf` (one page for each option) or `options-overview.png` (all five side by side).

Each page shows the logo on a light and a dark background, the colours, the typeface, the front and back of a business card, the mark on a book spine, the mark at small sizes and as an app icon, and a tagline. All five use typefaces that come with Borderless Studio and are free to use.

| | Option | The idea | Colours | Typeface | Tagline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Open Pages** | An open book whose right page has no edge: its lines of text run on past the page. Classic and literary. | Navy, gold, paper | EB Garamond | Books priced for readers. |
| 2 | **Horizon** | An open circle, a rising sun, and water running out through the gap. Modern, warm, friendly. | Deep teal, coral, sand | Source Sans 3 | Stories for every horizon. |
| 3 | **Bridge** | Fanned pages forming a bridge between two shores: Bangla and English, Bangladesh and Australia. Calm, trustworthy. | Delta green, ochre, cream | Crimson Pro | Books that bridge two worlds. |
| 4 | **Open B** | A classic serif B in a frame left open on one side. Looks like an established literary press; strongest on a spine. | Ink, vermilion, cream | Libre Baskerville | Good books, fairly priced. |
| 5 | **Paper Boat** | A boat folded from a page, as children fold them in the monsoon, on water that runs past the edge. Story led, gentle. | Monsoon indigo, saffron | EB Garamond italic | Stories that travel. |

Option 1 is the one the business kit currently uses. Once you choose, the whole set (logo files in every version, business card, letterhead, icons, social media images, brand guidelines and the Word files) is rebuilt in that option. You can also mix: for example the Bridge mark with the Open Pages typeface and colours, or another option's tagline.

## How to choose

- **Look at the spine and the smallest mark.** A publisher's logo is seen most often tiny, on a spine or a store thumbnail. Options 4 (Open B) and 1 (Open Pages) hold up best at that size.
- **Think of your list.** Literary fiction and memoir suit options 1 and 4; books that cross cultures and translations suit 3 and 5; a broad, modern list suits 2.
- **Check the name is free** before you commit (setup checklist, section 1), and search the image of your favourite on a trade mark register too: simple shapes such as a boat or an open book are common.

The drawings are in `source/options-lib.typ`; the boards are made by `source/boards.typ` and `source/overview.typ` with the Typst program that comes with Borderless Studio.
