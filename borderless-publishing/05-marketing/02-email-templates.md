# Email templates

Copy, fill in the brackets, and send. Keep each one short and kind.

## 1. Submission received

**Subject:** Received: [Title]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for sending us [Title]. It arrived safely. We read every submission ourselves and will reply within [three months]. If you accept an offer elsewhere in the meantime, please let us know.

With best wishes,
[Name], Borderless Publishing

## 2. Request for the full manuscript

**Subject:** [Title]: may we read the whole book?

Dear [Name],

We enjoyed the opening chapters of [Title] and would like to read the complete manuscript. Please send it as a Word file when you are ready. We aim to reply within [six weeks] of receiving it.

With best wishes,
[Name]

## 3. Offer to publish

**Subject:** An offer to publish [Title]

Dear [Name],

We would love to publish [Title]. [One or two sentences about what you admire in it.]

I attach our Author Publishing Agreement, which begins with a one page summary in plain words, together with our Reach First Pricing Policy and our guide for authors. In short: you pay nothing, you keep your copyright, you receive half of what we receive from every sale, and our prices are close to cost so that the book reaches as many readers as possible.

Please take your time, and do have the agreement checked by the Australian Society of Authors or a lawyer. I am happy to talk through any part of it.

With warm wishes,
[Name]

## 4. Decline

**Subject:** [Title]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for letting us read [Title]. After careful thought, we have decided it is not the right book for our list, and we are not able to make an offer. This is one judgement from one small publisher; please keep sending your work out.

With best wishes,
[Name]

## 5. Royalty statement

**Subject:** Royalty statement for [Title], [January to June / July to December] [year]

Dear [Name],

Attached is your royalty statement for [period]. [The payment of $____ has been made to your account today. / The amount of $____ is under $20 and has been carried forward to your next statement.]

[Number] copies were sold in this period across all formats. [One sentence of good news if there is any: a library order, a review, an event.]

As always, ask me for the store reports behind any line.

With thanks,
[Name]

## 6. Publication day

**Subject:** [Title] is published today

Dear [Name],

Today [Title] is out in the world. Here are the links to share:

- Amazon: [link]
- Apple Books, Kobo and others: [link]
- Bookshops and libraries can order it through Ingram with ISBN [__________].

Your author copies are on their way. Thank you for your trust and your work. Let us celebrate.

[Name]

## 7. Review request

**Subject:** Review copy: [Title] by [Author]

Dear [Name],

I am writing from Borderless Publishing, an independent publisher in Sydney. On [date] we publish [Title] by [Author], [one sentence about the book]. Given your interest in [their area], I wonder whether you would like a review copy, as an ebook or in print.

The book is priced at [AUD ____] in print and [____] as an ebook, close to cost, and our share of any margin goes to Blessings Foundation Bangladesh.

The press release and cover are attached. Thank you for considering it.

With best wishes,
[Name]

## 8. Library suggestion (for authors and readers to send to their library)

**Subject:** Book suggestion: [Title] by [Author]

Hello,

I would like to suggest a book for the library's collection: [Title] by [Author] (Borderless Publishing, [year]), ISBN [__________]. It is available to libraries through Ingram and other library suppliers. [One sentence on why local readers would enjoy it.]

Thank you,
[Name], library member
