# Launch checklist for each book

Use it alongside the publishing checklist in Borderless Studio (Publish, Checklist), which covers the files and the stores. This one covers telling people.

## Three months before publication

- [ ] Final cover ready; make the quote cards and sell sheet in Borderless Studio (Publish, Marketing).
- [ ] Ebook available for pre-order on Amazon and through Draft2Digital, with the publication date.
- [ ] Advance reader copies built (Export, Samples and advance copies) and sent to [10 to 20] reviewers, bloggers and book clubs.
- [ ] Ask two or three people for a short endorsement for the listing and the back cover of later printings.
- [ ] Contact local bookshops with the sell sheet; offer consignment where they prefer it.
- [ ] Contact festivals, libraries and community groups (including Bangladeshi Australian associations for Bangla and bilingual books) about events.

## One month before

- [ ] Press release written and sent to local papers, community radio (including Bangla language programs on SBS and community radio), and relevant newsletters.
- [ ] Launch event booked (bookshop, library or community hall), with copies ordered in time.
- [ ] Newsletter announcement drafted.
- [ ] Sample chapter on the website (Export, Samples).

## Publication week

- [ ] Check every store listing: price, description, cover, "Look inside".
- [ ] Publication day email to the author (email template 6) and to the newsletter.
- [ ] Share the quote cards, one each day for a week.
- [ ] Ask readers who enjoy it to leave an honest review and to suggest it to their library (email template 8).

## Within a month after

- [ ] Legal deposit done (one month after publication at the latest).
- [ ] Lending rights and Copyright Agency registrations done.
- [ ] Thank everyone who helped.
- [ ] Note what worked for the next book.
