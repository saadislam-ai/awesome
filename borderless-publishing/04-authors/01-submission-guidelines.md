# Submitting your book to Borderless Publishing

*For the website.*

Borderless Publishing is a small independent publisher in Sydney. We publish fiction and nonfiction in English and Bangla, and translations between them. We price our books as close to cost as booksellers allow, so that they reach as many readers as possible.

## What we are looking for

- Novels, short story collections, memoir and narrative nonfiction.
- Books in English or Bangla, and books that cross between cultures, especially between Bangladesh and Australia.
- Translations from Bangla into English, and from English into Bangla, where the translator has the original author's support.
- Completed manuscripts only.

We do not publish: poetry collections [at present], children's picture books, academic theses, books that promote hatred, or books written mostly by artificial intelligence.

## How it works for you

- **You pay nothing.** We never charge authors for editing, covers or publishing. A publisher that asks you for money is selling you a service, not publishing your book.
- **You keep your copyright.** You give us a licence to publish for five years, renewable, and you can take your rights back if the book stops selling.
- **You choose how your book is priced and shared**, from five models: a small margin shared 50/50 with us, all of the margin to you, selling at cost so that nobody makes a profit, a free ebook, or open access so anyone can share it. Statements come every six months.
- **Low prices, wide reach.** Because our prices are close to cost, the money per copy is small. Please read our Reach First Pricing Policy before you submit, so there are no surprises.
- **No advance** is paid.

## What to send

Email [submissions@your-domain] with the subject "Submission: [Title]" and attach:

1. **A cover letter** in the email: who you are, the title, the genre, the length in words, and why you would like to publish with us.
2. **A synopsis** of one page, telling the whole story including the ending (or, for nonfiction, what the book covers and why you are the person to write it).
3. **The first three chapters** (or about 10,000 words), as a Word or PDF file.
4. For a translation: a sample of the translation, the same pages in the original, and a note on who holds the rights in the original.

Please tell us if you have used artificial intelligence tools to write or edit any part of the book; it does not rule you out, but booksellers ask us about it.

## What happens next

- We confirm that your submission arrived within [one week].
- We reply to every submission within [three months]. If we would like to read the whole manuscript, we will ask.
- If we would like to publish, we send you our agreement and a summary in plain words, and we encourage you to have it checked by the Australian Society of Authors or a lawyer before you sign.

Simultaneous submissions to other publishers are fine; please tell us if you accept another offer.
