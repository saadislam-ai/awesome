# Five ways to publish your book with us

*Give this to every author with the agreement. The model you choose is written into Schedule 1 of the agreement.*

Every Borderless book is priced close to cost so that it reaches as many readers as possible. Within that, you choose how far to go: from a small margin that we share, to selling at cost so that nobody makes a profit, to giving the ebook away and letting readers share it.

In every model: **you pay nothing**, we pay for editing, cover, typesetting, ISBNs and distribution, and **you keep your copyright**.

## The five models

| | Model | Who it suits | What the reader pays | What you receive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Shared** | Most authors | The lowest price each store allows, plus about one dollar | Half of what we receive |
| 2 | **Author first** | Authors who want every cent of the margin | The same as Model 1 | All of what we receive |
| 3 | **At cost** | Authors who want the lowest possible price everywhere | The lowest price each store allows | Anything left over (usually little or nothing) |
| 4 | **Free ebook** | Authors who want readers anywhere to read the ebook free | Ebook free where stores allow it; print at cost | Anything left over |
| 5 | **Open access** | Authors who want their book shared as widely as possible, for example for readers in countries where books are hard to buy | As Model 4, and anyone may share the ebook free | Anything left over |

## Things to know before you choose

- **Even "at cost" is not free in print.** Paper, printing and the store's share must be paid by someone, so a printed book always costs the reader something. The ebook is where a price can go to nothing.
- **Amazon will not let us set a free ebook directly.** It sometimes matches a free price it finds at other stores, but it is not guaranteed. Apple Books, Kobo, Barnes and Noble and most library services through Draft2Digital accept free ebooks.
- **Open access cannot be undone.** In Model 5 you give everyone the right to copy and share your book free, unchanged and not for money, under the Creative Commons licence CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Copies already shared stay shared even if you change your mind later. You keep your copyright and the right to sell or license it in other ways. Please talk to the Australian Society of Authors or a lawyer before choosing it.
- **Free and cheap books still count.** Library lending rights, reviews and word of mouth do not depend on the price. Many authors find that a free or very cheap first book brings readers to their next one.
- **You can change.** Once a year either of us can propose another model; it changes when we both agree in writing (except that open access cannot be withdrawn for copies already shared).
