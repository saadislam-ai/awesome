# Choose your margin: how your book is priced and shared

*Give this to every author with the agreement. Your choices are written into Schedule 1 of the agreement.*

Every Borderless book starts at the lowest price each store allows, so that it reaches as many readers as possible. On top of that lowest price, **you choose your margin per copy, and we choose ours.** Either of us can choose any amount, including nothing at all.

In every case: **you pay nothing**, we pay for editing, cover, typesetting, ISBNs and distribution, and **you keep your copyright**.

## How it works

1. **You choose the Author's Margin**: what you would like from each copy. Common amounts are $0.50, $1, $2, $3 or $5. You can also choose **nothing**.
2. **We choose the Publisher's Margin**: what Borderless keeps from each copy to pay for the next book. It can also be **nothing**.
3. **The price** of each edition is the lowest the store allows, plus both margins, rounded up to a usual price ending such as .99.
4. **What a copy earns is shared in proportion to the two margins.** If you chose $3 and we chose $1, you receive three quarters of what each copy earns. If neither of us takes a margin, anything a copy still earns is yours.
5. **Optional: a free ebook.** The ebook is free wherever a store allows it, and at its lowest price where it does not.
6. **Optional: open access.** The ebook is free, and anyone may also share it under a Creative Commons licence.

## Common choices

| Choice | Your margin | Our margin | Who it suits | Your share of what a copy earns |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shared** | $0.50 | $0.50 | Most authors | Half |
| **Author first** | $1 | Nothing | Authors who want every cent | All of it |
| **Author earns more** | $3 | $1 | Authors who want a real income from each copy | Three quarters |
| **At cost: nobody earns** | Nothing | Nothing | Authors who want the lowest possible price everywhere | Anything left over (usually little or nothing) |
| **Free ebook** | Nothing | Nothing | Authors who want readers anywhere to read the ebook free | Anything left over |
| **Open access** | Nothing | Nothing | Authors who want their book shared as widely as possible, for example for readers where books are hard to buy | Anything left over |

Any other amounts are fine. Our pricing program shows the price and both shares for every store before anything is published.

Why does a copy sometimes earn a little more than the two margins added together? Prices are rounded up to .99, and on Amazon a paperback at $13.99 or more earns a higher royalty rate than one just below it. Whatever a copy really earns is shared in the same proportion.

## Things to know before you choose

- **Even "at cost" is not free in print.** Paper, printing and the store's share must be paid by someone, so a printed book always costs the reader something. The ebook is where a price can go to nothing.
- **Amazon will not let us set a free ebook directly.** It sometimes matches a free price it finds at other stores, but it is not guaranteed. Apple Books, Kobo, Barnes and Noble and most library services through Draft2Digital accept free ebooks.
- **Open access cannot be undone.** You give everyone the right to copy and share your book free, unchanged and not for money, under the Creative Commons licence CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Copies already shared stay shared even if you change your mind later. You keep your copyright and the right to sell or license it in other ways. Please talk to the Australian Society of Authors or a lawyer before choosing it.
- **Free and cheap books still count.** Library lending rights, reviews and word of mouth do not depend on the price. Many authors find that a free or very cheap first book brings readers to their next one.
- **You can always earn less.** You can lower your margin, even to nothing, at any time by telling us in writing. Raising a margin, or changing the free ebook choice, needs both of us to agree and can be proposed once a year.
