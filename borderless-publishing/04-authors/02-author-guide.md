# Publishing with Borderless: a guide for authors

*Give this to every author when they sign.*

Thank you for trusting us with your book. This guide explains what happens from signing to publication and after, who does what, and when you will hear from us.

## The road to publication

| Step | What happens | Who | Usual time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Signing | Both sign the agreement. You fill in the Author Information Form. | Both | Week 0 |
| 2. Final manuscript | You send the finished manuscript, author photograph and biography. | You | By the date in the agreement |
| 3. Structural read | We read the whole book and send a letter about structure, pace and clarity. | Us | 4 to 6 weeks |
| 4. Revision | You revise. | You | 4 to 8 weeks |
| 5. Copyedit | We correct spelling, grammar and consistency, as tracked changes. You accept or reject each one. | Both | 4 weeks |
| 6. Cover | We share two or three cover ideas and ask for your view. | Us, with you | Alongside the copyedit |
| 7. Typesetting | We lay out the pages for each edition. | Us | 1 to 2 weeks |
| 8. Proofs | You read the typeset pages for errors only. | You | 3 weeks |
| 9. Listing | We write the description and keywords with you, and set the prices. | Both | 2 weeks |
| 10. Printed proof | We order a printed copy and check it. | Us | 2 weeks |
| 11. Publication | The book goes on sale in every format and store. | Us | Publication day |

From final manuscript to publication usually takes [six to nine] months.

## What we do

- Editing, proofreading, cover design and typesetting, at our cost.
- ISBNs, barcodes, files for every store, and checks that they meet each store's rules.
- Legal deposit with the National Library, lending rights registration and Copyright Agency registration.
- Store listings, prices, and making the book available to bookshops and libraries worldwide.
- A royalty statement and payment every six months.
- A launch plan with you, and marketing material: a one page sheet for bookshops and libraries, quote cards for social media, and a press release.

## What we ask of you

- Answer queries and return proofs by the dates we agree, or tell us early if you cannot.
- Tell us about any material in the book that belongs to someone else, so we can get permission.
- Tell us about real people who could recognise themselves in the book, especially in memoir.
- Tell us if you used artificial intelligence tools in writing the book.
- Help launch the book in the ways that suit you: events, your own networks, local media.

## Money

What you receive depends on the publishing model you chose (see "Five ways to publish your book with us"): half or all of what we receive for each copy after the bookseller's share and the printing cost, or nothing at all if you chose to sell at cost or give the ebook away. Because our prices are close to cost, it is a small amount per copy in every model. Statements cover January to June and July to December, and are sent within 60 days. Amounts under 20 dollars are carried to the next statement, and everything is paid at least once a year.

You may buy copies of your book at our printing cost, plus postage, to sell at events. You keep what you make on those sales.

## Your rights

You keep your copyright. Our licence runs for five years from publication and then from year to year. If the book sells fewer than 25 copies in a year, or if we do not do what we promised, you can take your rights back; there are letters for this in our agreement pack.
