# Borderless Publishing: one page plan

## Mission

Publish good books, in English and Bangla, at prices close to what it costs to make them, so that they reach as many readers as possible.

## What makes it different

- **Reach first.** Prices are set at the lowest the platforms allow plus a small margin (see the Reach First Pricing Policy). Most publishers set prices for profit; we set them for readers.
- **Authors never pay.** We pay for editing, covers, typesetting, ISBNs and distribution, and share what each book earns 50:50 with its author.
- **Open with authors.** Every author can see the store reports behind their royalty statement.
- **Two languages.** Bangla and English books, and translations between them.

## Who it is for

- Readers who cannot afford usual book prices, in Australia, Bangladesh and wherever books are sold online.
- Authors, especially Bangladeshi and Australian authors and authors writing across both cultures, who want their book read more than they want an advance.
- Libraries, which pay authors and publishers through lending rights.

## How books reach readers

| Channel | Formats | Reaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon KDP | Ebook, paperback, hardcover | Amazon stores worldwide |
| IngramSpark | Paperback, hardcover, ebook | Bookshops and libraries worldwide through Ingram |
| Draft2Digital | Ebook | Apple, Kobo, Barnes and Noble, library services |
| Direct | Paperback | Events, local bookshops on consignment |

All files are made in Borderless Studio and checked against each platform's rules before upload.

## Risks and what we do about them

| Risk | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Low prices mean little income to repay costs | Keep costs per title low; use Borderless Studio for typesetting and covers; pay freelancers fairly but only for what each book needs |
| Platform rules change | Spec files are reviewed every six months; the app warns when they are old |
| Legal claim over a book's content | Author warranties, legal read for risky nonfiction, insurance once publishing others |
| Too much work for one person | Clear yearly calendar; templates for every letter; accept only as many books as can be done well |
