# Donation and Transparency Policy

*For the website. Have the wording checked by your accountant and a lawyer before publishing it (see the setup checklist, section 3).*

## Our commitment

Borderless Publishing is a small business in Sydney, Australia. It is not a charity. It donates its share of the margin from book sales to **Blessings Foundation Bangladesh**, [one sentence describing the Foundation's work, as approved by the Foundation].

## What is donated

For each year ending 31 December:

| Line | How it is worked out |
| --- | --- |
| Money received from book sales | What the stores and distributors paid us, after their share and the printing cost |
| Less: authors' royalties | Half of the money received for each author's books |
| Less: direct publishing costs | Editing, covers, proof copies, legal deposit copies, platform fees, bank fees |
| **= Margin donated** | Everything that remains, donated to Blessings Foundation Bangladesh |

If direct costs are larger than the money received in a year, nothing is donated that year and the shortfall is carried to the next year. We do not pay ourselves a salary from book sales.

## When

Once a year, by 31 March, for the year before.

## How you can check

Every year we publish a statement on our website with the figures above, the date of each donation, and a confirmation from the Foundation that it received the money.

## Authors

Authors receive half of the money received for their books. An author may choose to give some or all of their share to the Foundation too; that is their choice and is shown on their royalty statement.
