# Reach First Pricing Policy

*For the website, and to give every author with their contract.*

Most books are priced to make as much money as possible. Ours are priced to reach as many readers as possible.

## How we set a price

1. For each edition and each store, we find the lowest price the store allows. For printed books that is usually the printing cost plus the store's share; for ebooks it is the store's minimum.
2. We add a small margin of [about one Australian dollar, or its equivalent] per copy, and round to a normal looking price.
3. We use the same method for every book and every author. We do not raise prices when a book sells well.

Borderless Studio, the program we use to make our books, works out these prices from each store's published rules, and we check them against the stores' own calculators before publishing.

## Where the money goes

From each copy sold, the store keeps its share and the printer is paid. What remains is the margin. Half of the margin goes to the author. From our half we repay the direct costs of making the book (editing, cover, proofs), and what remains keeps the press going and pays for the next book.

## What this means

- Readers pay close to what the book costs to make.
- Authors receive less per copy than at usual prices, and we tell them so before they sign. In return, their book can reach more readers, and they keep their copyright.
- Prices can change when a store's printing costs or rules change. We review prices at least twice a year.

## Exceptions

An author may ask us to set a higher price for their book, for example to match the other books in a series. We will discuss it and agree it in writing.
